# Opportunity Unifying Resource Plans (OUR Plans)

**Status:** Draft for review — plans, honestly labelled as plans
**Licence:** CC BY-SA 4.0 (same as the rest of TriSex.org)
**Live companion page:** `/our-plans`

## What this is

OUR is one umbrella — the **Opportunity Unifying Resource Plans** — read four ways.
Each reading is a pillar describing what happens when someone forks TriSex.org.
Nothing here claims existing programs, funding, or partner organizations. What
already exists and works is listed under "What is real today"; everything else is
a commitment we hold ourselves — and any fork carrying the OUR name — to.

## The four pillars

### 1. Opportunity Unity Reality
Every fork is an opportunity, every adopter adds unity, and honesty keeps it reality.

- **Opportunity:** anyone may fork this app and its data models under CC BY-SA 4.0 —
  no permission needed, no gatekeeping.
- **Unity:** forks stay connected through the adopter registry and federated
  syndication rather than central control.
- **Reality:** no fabricated partnerships, capacity numbers, or member counts —
  here or in any fork that carries the OUR name.

### 2. Open United Resources
The whole resource base — source code, schemas, the 86-variation catalogue, fold
sequences, wiki content — is open and shared alike, so united communities never
start from zero.

- Full source and database schema are copyable via Remix; the Inclusive Ordering
  Framework is a documented public surface.
- Share-alike: derivative designs and data stay CC BY-SA 4.0, so improvements
  flow back to everyone.
- Resources are pooled, not hoarded — the LETS mutual-credit framing applies to
  knowledge as much as goods.

### 3. Our Universal Resilience
A movement that lives in many independent copies cannot be shut down, bought out,
or quietly rewritten. Forks are the resilience plan.

- No single point of failure: if this instance disappears, every fork keeps the
  catalogue, the wiki, and the cooperative model alive.
- Regional chapters, translations, and specialized-community forks are
  encouraged, not merely tolerated.
- Privacy-preserving verification (passkeys, wallet age checks) travels with the
  code — resilience includes member safety.

### 4. Obviously Unitarian Research
Research here is unitarian in the plain sense: one equal community of
cooperators, no role hierarchy, no researcher/subject divide — and it is obvious
because every method is public.

- Every cooperator is an equal contributor; there are no admin, clinic-staff, or
  consumer castes anywhere in the data model.
- Evidence claims cite public sources (NIH NLM, NCCIH) and design hypotheses are
  labelled as hypotheses, never as medical advice.
- Findings, schemas, and methods are published in the open so any fork can
  replicate, challenge, or extend them.

## What is real today (fork paths that already work)

1. **Fork everything** — Remix the whole platform on Replit (`/remix-replit`):
   full source, database schema, wiki content, cooperative tooling.
2. **Fork the framework** — Adopt only the Inclusive Ordering Framework
   (`/fork-the-framework`): versioned v1.0.0, CC BY-SA 4.0, stable public API for
   the variation catalogue, fitting params, and multi-use balance schema.
3. **Self-report** — Register a fork or adoption at `/inclusive-ordering-registry`
   (entries stay "pending" until manually verified; the registry is honestly
   empty until real adopters submit).

## What is explicitly NOT claimed

- No OUR organization, nonprofit, cooperative filing, or bank account exists.
- No funding, grants, or partner organizations are attached to these plans.
- No fork is endorsed or verified beyond what the adopter registry honestly shows.
